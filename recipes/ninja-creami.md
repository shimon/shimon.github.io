## Pineapple Dole Whip

**Status: Package-scaled; Creami texture and tasting results pending.**

### Ingredients

- **111 g DOLE pineapple soft-serve powder**, or **½ level cup** (approximately 112 g using our label)
- **1½ US cups water** (about 355 mL)

Our bag calls for **4.4 lb powder with 6.75 US quarts water**, and lists **2 tablespoons powder = 28 g**. The scaled ratio is approximately **74 g powder per US cup water**. These measurements apply to this package, not every pineapple powder.

### Method

1. Whisk powder and water in a separate jug until fully dissolved. Start with just these ingredients: no extra sugar, pudding powder, or coconut milk.
2. Assemble the Swirl container for freezing according to its manual, including closing the bottom nozzle. Pour no higher than its MAX FILL line. Reserve excess; do not dilute the mix to reach the line.
3. Fit the storage lid and freeze upright on a level surface for **at least 24 hours**.
4. As an experimental starting program, select **SOFT SERVE → FRUIT WHIP**. This applies Ninja's fruit-soft-serve program to commercial mix; it is not a manufacturer-tested recipe for this powder.
5. If crumbly, try **RE-SPIN without added liquid first**. If still dry, try a small splash of water and record how much. Too much liquid can make it runny.
6. Fit the dispensing lid and dispense according to the machine instructions. Serve immediately.

**Next experiment:** Establish the plain baseline first. If texture is good but pineapple flavor is weak, replace a measured portion of the water with pineapple juice in a separate trial.

**Dietary check:** Verify the actual powder's ingredient/allergen label for dairy and gluten, particularly if repackaged by a third-party seller.

## Processing and planning notes

- Use the exact model's manual for assembly, fill limits, freezing, and program selection. A scoopable program is not interchangeable with a dedicated soft-serve program.
- For dispensing, use the appropriate **SOFT SERVE** program. The Swirl dispenses from its specially assembled container; there is no separate chute to load with scooped ice cream.
- Freeze level for **at least 24 hours** before the initial spin. Never spin a tilted frozen base.
- For a crumbly first spin, consider RE-SPIN before adding liquid. For fully refrozen leftovers, follow the manual's reprocessing instructions rather than automatically choosing RE-SPIN.
- Spin and dispense near serving time, not an hour in advance.
- With six containers, run a tasting round, transfer leftovers to labeled freezer-safe storage, then wash and reuse the containers for revisions. Transfer back and refreeze level if the manual permits reprocessing; don't spin an irregular transferred frozen block.
- Allow a full freeze window between each prep and tasting. For Saturday afternoon service, final preparation on Thursday gives a useful buffer. Friday evening preparation is too late for Saturday morning processing.
