I'm a software product guy. I've worked as a product manager and engineer in big and small companies, freelanced, started a company of my own, and launched dozens of products. I'm currently [on a sabbatical](/blog/linkedin/2026/08/06/after-7-5-years-at-verily-im-taking-a-sabbatical.html), which means I'm exploring some new projects and ideas but also taking more time to focus on my family life. I do a bit of consulting work, especially in the area of technology for biomedical research.

I previously worked on:
 - [Verily Workbench](https://verily.com/workbench), as it grew from idea into Verily's leading business, including closely working with [the *All of Us* Research Program](https://support.researchallofus.org/hc/en-us/articles/360039175232-Intro-to-the-All-of-Us-Researcher-Workbench)
 - [Danger Fort Labs](https://www.dangerfort.com/), a consultancy where I helped large and small companies get better at product discovery
 - Happie, a company that aimed to make hiring more fun and efficient (as CTO)
 - [PatientsLikeMe](http://patientslikeme.com), where I led the Open Research Exchange, a system for rapidly developing and testing Patient Reported Outcome Measures
 - [Smarterer](http://smarterer.com), a popular skills testing offering where I was the first product manager
 - I was a founder of NodeRabbit which became [AppSembler](https://appsembler.com/).
